# Vision

## Elevator pitch

You're a brand-new Junior Perimeter Technician on a cheerful corporate frontier colony. Your job is to keep the adorable local "pests" out of the hydroponic domes. Over a campaign of increasingly desperate tower defense battles, you learn the truth about the pests, the company, and the shimmering mineral everyone is mining. It all starts with space-lettuce. It ends with the fate of a world.

## Target experience

- **Hour 1:** Delight. Bright colors, silly enemies, jokes in the corporate memos, satisfying tower pops. Easy to learn.
- **Midgame:** Curiosity and unease. Strategic depth opens up (loadout puzzles, enemy counters), and the story starts asking questions.
- **Late game:** Investment and dread. Hard maps that demand mastery. Characters you care about are in real danger.
- **Finale:** Catharsis. A last stand that uses everything you've learned and everyone you've allied with. Bittersweet, then hopeful.

## Pillars

1. **Story with heart.** Funny first, devastating later, hopeful at the end. The emotional arc is carried by mechanics as well as dialogue (for example, a former enemy race's creatures later become your towers).
2. **Meaningful loadouts.** Towers are collected across the campaign, but only 4 come into each map. The choice should feel like a puzzle, not a formality.
3. **Readable depth.** Every enemy ability has a clear visual tell and a clear counter. Losses should teach.
4. **Vibrant colors, deep darks.** Saturated, glowing pixel art against rich, inky shadow. The overall palette shifts across acts, from sunny pastel to dusk to void.
5. **Fair challenge.** Each map is harder than the last, introduces something new, and is beatable with smart play rather than grinding. Grinding helps, but isn't required on the default difficulty.

## Tone ramp

| Act | Tone | Comedy | Stakes | Palette |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | Whimsical, goofy | High (memos, QUILL's jokes, squeaky enemies) | A lettuce harvest | Sunny pastels, bright greens and pinks |
| II | Adventurous, uneasy | Medium (jokes start to sour) | The colony, a first-contact war | Golden hour into dusk, ambers and violets |
| III | Serious, desperate | Low (gallows humor, rare relief) | A world, everyone you love | Void purples, alarm reds, deep blacks with bright highlights |
| Finale | Cathartic, hopeful | A returning callback | Everything | Dawn through the dark |

## Scope (current plan)

- About 17 campaign maps across 3 acts plus a finale. We can expand this if the story needs more room.
- About 12–16 towers in total, each with an in-map upgrade path (tiers 1–3, then a choice of 2 specializations at tier 4).
- About 20–30 enemy types with distinct abilities, plus bosses.
- Post-game: challenge maps, veteran modifiers, endless mode.
- Browser-only, desktop first, saves in localStorage.

## Non-goals (for now)

- Multiplayer, accounts, or a backend.
- Monetization.
- Fully branching narrative. We use a few key choices with felt payoffs and one meaningful ending variation.
