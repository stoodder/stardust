## FIN · Clearing the Way

*Walk them home. The choir sings the jingle as a funeral hymn, and your counter counts down to zero.*

- **Facts:** The Long Walk, from Dome Row north over Okonkwo's Gap to the mouth of the Deep Halls · `finale` (Act III dark; the eastern sky warms as waves clear; dawn in Phase 3) · **Choir escort** → the Leaving Song → Dawn · 22×14, 21 buildable (Halls-mouth ledges as high ground; natural gold seams as power nodes; four waymark lanterns as wreckage) · 26 waves (Phase 1: 18; Phase 2: 8 add waves on a fixed cadence) · 900 credits · three spawns (west rift field, east Bore road, north Halls mouth) + air lanes + rifts; the road is 2-wide · meter **Voices** (`meterLabel: 'voices'`: choir members still singing) · companion: **the player picks who walks with them** from Ves'thara (Stillness, default), Bramble (Underfoot), Zibzab (Clearance Sale), and QUILL (Time Dilation; absent under Purge) · Technician: Suppressing Fire (par; under Keep, Time Dilation is also equippable here, for Phase 1 only; the Technician slot can't take the picked companion's own ability) · allies: the choir (Pebble; Old Thrum if spared), the Returned (if `returned`), colonist militia (if `convoy`) · **Deep Bore** secondary timer in Phase 1 · a **checkpoint at the start of Phase 2** (towers, credits, Voices, and Bore progress are restored; using it forfeits Perfect and Challenge for that run; Results offers "Retry from the Halls"). On this map only, the latest checkpoint persists to the save, so Continue offers "Resume the Long Walk".
- **Story purpose:** The third tear beat (the countdown), then catharsis and the one returning laugh. **Target: grief turning into ceremony, then hope.**

**Layout.** The road starts at the A1M1 gate on the south edge and winds north. At mid-map it climbs past the ridge above Okonkwo's Gap, where the **memorial wreck** stands: the wrecked variant of `memorialTower`, tooltip showing the `{name}` plate. The east band holds the Deep Bore rig and its progress bar. The north edge is the Halls mouth, a wide ledge where the choir stops and sings. Lighting starts at full dark. Each Phase 1 wave cleared warms the eastern **sky** one step (the backdrop only); the darkness over the map and its grade don't lift until Dawn's light lands.

**Waves.**
- *Phase 1, the Descent (W1–18):* 1 Motes ahead of the choir · 2 Fractures from the rift field · 3 Rift Moths · 4 Bore security: an Auditor with its drone escort (they guard the Bore, and the road runs past it) · 5 Maws · 6 Mote clusters with a pair of Undertows · 7 Silencers · 8 gunships over the road · 9 the choir passes the memorial (cue; no wave pressure for 10 s) · 10 Hollow from all three spawns · 11 a Fracture column, and behind it a Bore crew (Associates) with two Black Budget Operatives · 12 Maws + Silencers · 13 Rift Moth flock · 14 Associates and one Liability on the Bore road, behind a Mote screen · 15 rifts everywhere · 16 Fractures with an Undertow or two · 17 the largest surge · 18 the Halls mouth: everything converges. (If the Bore fills before the choir arrives, its breakthrough surge spawns here.) The finale requires air, jam, and splash; armored, cloaked, and burrowing units are soft here, kept ≤ 15% of any wave's threat. Phase 1 kills behave as in Act III: nothing scatters yet.
- *Phase 2, the Unsung (W19–26):* it starts at the later of W18 cleared and the choir reaching the Halls mouth; the checkpoint is taken there. The Unsung rises from the Halls (gathers → splits → phases and burrows, surfacing among the singers → the Song, with Silencer adds; `layToRest` at rest progress 100%). Add waves arrive every ~20 s. Hollow broken inside the song radius are laid to rest (gold motes; the counter falls); outside it they scatter and re-form once. Under Keep, Directive 0 fires 20 s in, as the choir steps into the Halls. Target length ~3 minutes at 1x, set at Keep's ×1.25 rest multiplier; every `quill_choice` must land within ±30% of it (Purge runs about 25% longer, Rewrite about 17% shorter; [maps.md §5.5](../../design/maps.md#55-the-leaving-song-finale-rules)).
- *Phase 3, Dawn:* no waves. At rest progress 100% every live Hollow is laid to rest, hushed singers wake, and the light comes up from the east over ~8 s; the map is won as it lands.

**Medals.** Victory · Perfect (no singer hushed) · **Laid to Rest:** in Phase 2, at least 90% of Hollow units destroyed are destroyed inside the song. **Objective Intel +1, Waymarks:** relight all four waymark lanterns (clear their wreckage) in Phase 1.
**Rewards.** Stardust 500 · Intel 3 (+1).
**Par build.** Mindspike, Hum Choir, Sproing Pad, Scuttle Den; companion Ves'thara; Technician Suppressing Fire. Mindspikes take both specs: Echo Spike on the road for the Mote and Fracture swarms, Dread at the Halls mouth for the Silencers' tells.
