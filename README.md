# Stardust

A web-based **2D isometric pixel-art tower defense game** with a story that begins whimsical and funny and grows genuinely moving. You're a rookie Junior Perimeter Technician on Everglow Dynamics' cheerful frontier colony, keeping adorable "pests" out of the lettuce domes, until you learn what the pests are running from, and what the shimmering stardust really is.

- Build towers on a predefined grid. Bring only **4 tower types** into each map.
- In-map upgrades with tier-4 specializations, plus permanent upgrades earned across the campaign.
- 18 campaign maps across three acts and a finale, 16 towers from four factions, 27 enemies, and 6 bosses.

**Status:** Milestone M0 (design, story, and technical docs) is complete. M1 (a playable vertical slice of map A1M1) is next.
